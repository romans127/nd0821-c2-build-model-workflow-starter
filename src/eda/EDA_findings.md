# EDA findings — sample1.csv (20,000 rows)

Price is heavily right-skewed: median $105 and mean $153, but the max reaches $10,000 and 5 listings are priced at $0; the 95th percentile is $350, so the configured min_price=10 / max_price=350 band trims the junk at both tails (~5 low, ~994 high rows). All coordinates fall inside the NYC bounding box (lon -74.25..-73.50, lat 40.5..41.2), so no geographic outliers need removal in this sample. `last_review` loads as object (string) and must be converted to datetime. Missingness is concentrated in `last_review` and `reviews_per_month` (4,123 rows each — listings with no reviews yet), while `name` (7) and `host_name` (8) have only a handful of gaps. These four review-related gaps plus the price band are the main targets for the basic_cleaning step.
